BOB'S FRIENDS

KEVIN

Kevin is a tall, two-eyed Minion with a small sprout of hair on top of his head. He's brave, responsible, and naturally takes on a leadership role among the other Minions.

Stuart

Stuart is a short, round, yellow Minion with a small black eye and a small black nose. He is known for his love of music and his ability to play the guitar.

The friendship between Stuart, Kevin, and Bob is pure chaotic joy. Each of them brings a completely different energy—Kevin is the determined leader, Stuart is the laid-back troublemaker, and Bob is the sweet, childlike heart of the group. Together, they turn even the simplest task into a wild adventure full of accidents, laughter, and unexpected heroics. Their bond works because they balance each other perfectly: Kevin keeps them moving forward, Stuart adds unpredictability, and Bob reminds them why they’re doing it in the first place. No matter how messy things get, their loyalty and love for one another always shine through, making their friendship both hilariously chaotic and genuinely heartwarming.
